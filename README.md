# RedlineOS

**A lightweight, gaming-focused operating system. Fast by default, yours to customize, and free of spyware.**

> ⚠️ RedlineOS is in early development. Features listed below are planned unless marked as done.

---

## Why RedlineOS?

Gamers today are stuck choosing between two bad options:

- **Mainstream operating systems** that ship with telemetry, ads, bloatware, forced accounts and background services you never asked for, all eating into your performance and your privacy.
- **Linux distributions** that respect your privacy, but often expect you to spend hours in the terminal before you can launch a single game.

RedlineOS aims to be the third option: an OS that runs at the edge of your hardware's performance, gets you into your games fast, and lets you make every part of it your own, without collecting a single byte about you.

## Core principles

** Performance first**
Minimal background processes, a lean base system and gaming-focused tuning out of the box. Your hardware's power goes to your games, not to the OS.

** Customize everything**
Themes, layouts, panels, colors, fonts, animations. If you can see it, you should be able to change it, without editing config files by hand.

** No spyware. Ever.**
No telemetry, no ads, no tracking, no forced online accounts. What happens on your machine stays on your machine.

** Simple by default**
Sensible defaults and graphical tools for everything important. The terminal is there if you want it, never because you need it.

## Planned features

RedlineOS takes its naming from motorcycle racing:

| Component | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Livery** | Theme system. Swap the entire look of your desktop, just like a race bike's paint scheme. |
| **Paddock** | Central settings hub where you tune and set up your system. |
| **Quickshift** | Fast app and game launcher. No wasted time between you and your game. |
| **Powerband** | Performance mode that shifts system resources toward gaming when a game is running. |

Also planned:

- Gaming tools preinstalled and preconfigured (Steam, Proton, controller support)
- Up-to-date GPU drivers with easy setup
- Lightweight desktop with low RAM and CPU usage at idle
- Simple graphical installer
